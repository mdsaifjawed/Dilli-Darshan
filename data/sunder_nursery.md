## 2. Timings & Tickets
*   **Opening Hours:** **7:00 AM to 10:00 PM** (last entry at 9:00 PM or 9:30 PM).
*   **Weekly Off Day:** **Open every day of the year.**
*   **Entry Fees:**
    *   **Indian Citizens/SAARC Visitors:** **₹50** per person
    *   **Children (5-12 years):** **₹25** per person
    *   **Foreign Tourists:** **₹200** per person
    *   **Children under 5 years & Differently-abled visitors:** **Free entry**

## 3. How to Reach & Metro
*   **Nearest Metro Stations:**
    *   **JLN Stadium (Jawaharlal Nehru Stadium) Metro Station (Violet Line)**: Approximately **1.7 km to 2-3 km**.
    *   **Jangpura Metro Station (Violet Line)**: Around **600 meters to 800 meters** (about an 8-minute walk).
    *   **Khan Market Metro Station (Violet Line)**: Approximately **1.34 km to 2 km**.
    *   **Supreme Court Metro Station (Blue Line)**: Roughly **3 km**.
    *   **Hazrat Nizamuddin Metro Station (Pink Line)**: Particularly convenient as it's very close to the Hazrat Nizamuddin Railway Station, which is about **800m** from Sunder Nursery.
*   **Onward Journey & Transit Guidance:**
    *   From **JLN Stadium, Jangpura, Khan Market, or Supreme Court** metro stations, you can easily take an **auto-rickshaw or a cab** for a short ride directly to the park entrance.
    *   From **Hazrat Nizamuddin Metro Station**, exit from **Gate numbers 2 or 3** and find auto-rickshaws or cycle rickshaws to Sunder Nursery. The ride will typically be 5-10 minutes.

## 4. Nearby Iconic Food Spots
*   **Fabcafe by the Lake:** Located within Sunder Nursery, offering healthy food, desserts, and North Indian cuisine with serene lake views and outdoor seating.
*   **Carnatic Cafe Sunder Nursery:** A lakeside South Indian restaurant within the nursery, serving authentic dishes like Masala Dosa, Benne Dosa, and filter coffee in a self-service garden setting.
*   **Cortasso Café:** A pet-friendly cafe within the nursery, offering a cozy atmosphere.

## 5. Quick Reference & Facts
*   **Parking:** Available inside Sunder Nursery.
    *   **Car Parking:** **₹100** for 4 hours on weekdays; **₹200** for 4 hours on Sundays and gazetted holidays.
    *   **Two-wheelers:** **₹35** for 4 hours.
