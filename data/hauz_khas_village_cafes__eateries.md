# Hauz Khas Village Cafes & Eateries
## 1. History & Architecture
Hauz Khas Village, while a modern urban village, is historically significant, built around the remains of the Hauz Khas complex, which includes a water tank (Hauz-i-Alai), an Islamic seminary (Madrasa), a mosque, and Firoz Shah Tughlaq's tomb. These structures date back to the Delhi Sultanate period, particularly the reign of Alauddin Khalji (13th-14th century) and Firoz Shah Tughlaq (14th century). The village itself has evolved into a vibrant cultural and culinary hub, blending ancient ruins with a contemporary cafe and nightlife scene. The information below focuses on the diverse range of cafes and eateries rather than the detailed history of the monuments themselves.

## 2. Timings & Tickets
*   **Village Entry:** There is **no entry fee** for Hauz Khas Village itself.
*   **Cafes:** Generally open between **10 AM and 11 AM**.
*   **Bars:** Typically open around **6 PM**.
*   **Closing Hours:** Most establishments operate until **1 AM on Fridays and Saturdays**, and until **midnight on other days**.
*   **Cafe/Bar Specific Charges:**
    *   **Hauz Khas Social:** May have a cover charge of **₹500** on Friday/Saturday evenings.
    *   **Summer House Café:** Potential cover charge of **₹500-₹700** for live music nights.
    *   **Raasta:** Typically has a cover charge of **₹500-₹700**.
    *   **Naivedyam:** **Cash-only** restaurant.

## 3. How to Reach & Metro
*   **Nearest Delhi Metro Station:** **Green Park Metro Station**
*   **Metro Line:** Yellow Line
*   **Nearest Gate Number:** **Gate 1 or Gate 2**. From these gates, Hauz Khas Village is approximately a 15-20 minute walk or a short auto-rickshaw ride.
*   **Transit Guidance:** Exit from Green Park Metro Station Gate 1 or 2 and take an auto-rickshaw or walk towards Hauz Khas Village. The village entrance has a designated parking area, and the village itself is pedestrian-only.

## 4. Nearby Iconic Food Spots
Hauz Khas Village is a vibrant hub brimming with an eclectic mix of cafes, each offering a unique ambiance and culinary experience.

### For Specialty Coffee & Quiet Work
*   **Café OMG:** Quiet, European-style atmosphere, ideal for specialty coffee, breakfast, and laptop work. Flat white highly recommended. Expect **₹250-₹450** for coffee and food.
*   **Wishing Tree Café:** Offers specialty pour-overs from Indian single-origin beans. Limited seating; best for quieter weekday mornings. Coffee prices: **₹200-₹300** per cup.
*   **Blue Tokai Coffee Roasters:** A must-visit for coffee lovers, praised for quality and sustainability. Great spot for working with a laptop.
*   **Kunzum Travel Café:** Unique concept cafe for travelers.

### For Brunch & Dining with a View
*   **Social Hauz Khas:** Famous rooftop and excellent Sunday brunch (12 PM - 4 PM) with views of Hauz Khas Fort. Can get crowded; booking recommended for weekends. Expect **₹600-₹1,200** with drinks.
*   **Mia Bella:** Romantic European-style ambiance, known for Italian wood-fired pizzas, pastas, and terrace seating with fort and lake views. Perfect for date nights. Meal for two: **₹800-₹1,500**.
*   **Café Lota:** (Just outside the village) Serves modern Indian cuisine in a beautiful indoor and outdoor setting. Good for a sit-down lunch. Prices: **₹500-₹900** per person.
*   **Gunpowder:** Specializes in South Indian/Andhra cuisine with panoramic Hauz Khas lake views. Recommended for groups of 4-8; reservations advised. Costs: **₹400-₹700** per person.
*   **Coast Cafe:** Modern, minimal decor, bay windows, and pristine white setting. Known for coastal Indian cuisine (Californian tacos, Malabar paranthas, appams). Outdoor seating highly recommended.

### For Traditional & Regional Flavors
*   **Naivedyam:** Established in 1992, one of the oldest restaurants, offering South Indian thalis in a temple-themed setting. Dosa highly regarded. **Cash-only**. Prices: **₹150-₹350**.
*   **Yeti:** Great choice for Tibetan and Himalayan cuisine. Thenthuk (Tibetan noodle soup) popular during colder days. Expect prices around **₹250-₹450**.

### For Drinks, Nightlife & Live Music
*   **Hauz Khas Social:** Beyond brunch, a buzzing spot for cocktails, beers, and decent food. Gets packed by **9 PM on weekends**.
*   **Summer House Café:** Known for live music most nights, featuring indie and electronic acts.
*   **Raasta:** Offers Caribbean reggae vibes, atmosphere peaks between **11 PM and 1 AM**.

## 5. Quick Reference & Facts
*   **Key Highlights:** A unique blend of historical ruins and contemporary cafe, dining, and nightlife experiences. Known for its diverse culinary scene and artistic vibe.
*   **Recommended Duration:** **3-5 hours** for cafe hopping, dining, and enjoying the ambiance; longer if exploring the fort ruins or staying for nightlife.
*   **Photography Rules:** Generally permitted in public areas and within cafes (with permission). Be respectful of privacy.
*   **Visitor Tips:**
    *   **Parking:** Park at the **designated lot near the entrance (₹50)**, as the village itself is **pedestrian-only**.
    *   **Cash:** Carry **₹500-₹1,000 in cash** for spots like Naivedyam and some bars that might be cash-only. An ATM is available at the entrance.
    *   **Weather:** Outdoor seating is most pleasant from **November to March**. Be mindful of extreme heat from April to June and consider reserving indoor seating during the monsoon season (July-September).
    *   **Reservations:** For popular places like Mia Bella, Café Lota, and Gunpowder, especially on weekends, making a reservation is advisable. Sunday brunch at Social often sells out by Wednesday.
